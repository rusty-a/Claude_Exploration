Find the best fan spot

Tries about 150 spots and directions on a coarse grid, then checks the top 6 on a finer grid. Dots on the plan show each spot's best result.

Fans

Direction is a compass bearing in degrees: 0 east, 90 north, 180 west, 270 south. Drag fans, boxes and "You" on the plan.

Boxes

Openings

Opening is a window or door where air moves freely in or out. Vent pushes air in at a set speed.

Room

Origin is the south-west floor corner. x points east, y points north. Changing the room restarts the flow.

Heat

Give a box a heat output (a computer about 150 W, a TV about 100 W, a radiator 500 to 1500 W). A seated person gives about 70 to 100 W. Warm air rises, and air through an opening comes in at the outside temperature. Walls and furniture are heavy, so they stay at the temperature you set and give or take heat from the air next to them (3 W/m²K). Changing the start temperature restarts the flow.

Feels like is the air temperature at you minus the cooling effect of the air speed (ASHRAE 55, from the standard effective temperature). It assumes you sit (1.1 met) in light summer clothes (0.5 clo), 50 % humidity, and walls at the air temperature.

Model

Left out: walls that warm up or cool down over time, sun, and radiation. The grid has about 40 cells along the longest side. A fan is a disk of cells that pushes air at a set speed. Use the app to compare spots, not to promise a number.
